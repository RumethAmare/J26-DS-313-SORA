# S002 — Hospital channelling — booking a doctor appointment

**Speaker A:** Hemas Hospital — Ishani (woman)  
**Speaker B:** Customer — Ishani Karunaratne (woman)  
**Length:** ~1.8 min · 25 turns

**How numbers are said in this script:**
- English digits run together, no pauses
- Sinhala digits ONE AT A TIME, pause after each (…)

Read numbers **exactly as written** — word by word. `…` means a short pause (about half a second). Everything else: speak naturally; small changes are fine.
